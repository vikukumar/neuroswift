# Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, contributors and maintainers of NeuroSwift pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent and communicate clearly
- welcome feedback and differing technical views
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence
- help keep discussions useful and inclusive

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- personal attacks or intimidation
- trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- sharing private information without permission
- repeated bad-faith arguments after moderation

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces such as issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior through a private channel with enough detail to investigate responsibly.
